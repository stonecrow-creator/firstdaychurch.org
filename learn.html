The Teachings

The Church of the First Day offers a path of study and practice through three foundational works. Each opens another door into the life of the Practitioner, and each invites us to continue the work of becoming.

A foundational work of Dragon Magick, presenting the Twelve Laws and the principles through which the Practitioner learns to walk with awareness, courage, discipline, and purpose.
