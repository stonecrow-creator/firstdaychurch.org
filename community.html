Walking the Path Together

COMMUNITY

A place to gather, to share, to learn, and to walk the Path together.

You Do Not Walk Alone

The Path of the First Day is a personal journey, but it is not meant to be a lonely one. We learn from our own experience, from the living world, and from one another.

Gather

Come together with others who are exploring awakening, recognition, and becoming. Community begins simply—with people willing to meet one another where they are.

Learn

Share questions, insights, stories, and experiences. No one person possesses every answer. We grow by remaining curious and willing to learn.

Share

Bring what you have discovered into the world around you. The light we recognize within ourselves becomes more meaningful when we learn how to share it with others.

Come As You Are

You do not need to arrive with all the answers. You do not need to have walked the Path before. Come with your questions. Come with your hopes. Come with your doubts. Come as you are.

Service
